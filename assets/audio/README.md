Bundled alarm sounds.

Drop extra sound files in this folder (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.m4a` or
`.aac`) and rebuild the app. They appear automatically in every sound list,
named after the file: `morning_birds.mp3` shows as "Morning birds".

`classic.wav`, `gentle.wav` and `digital.wav` are the built-in tones; keep
them, because saved alarms refer to them.

To use a sound without rebuilding, pick "Choose audio file…" in any sound
list on the phone instead.
